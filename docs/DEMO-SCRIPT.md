# StoreAgent — demo video script

For the Shopify App Store submission. Target length **2:30**.

Narration is written in short sentences so it is easy to read aloud.
Every query below was tested against the live store and works.

---

## Before you press record

1. Hard-reload the storefront — **Ctrl+Shift+R**.
2. Run `flip flops` once and throw it away. This warms the search index.
3. Set inventory on at least one variant if you plan to show the cart.
   Everything is sold out right now, so add-to-cart cannot succeed.
4. Do not deploy within 10 minutes of recording. A turn that lands during a
   restart just fails.
5. Close other tabs so no notification sounds land in the take.

---

## SCENE 1 — The admin (0:00–0:30)

**Screen:** Shopify admin → Apps → StoreAgent

> "This is StoreAgent. It is a shopping assistant that answers customer
> questions using only the store's real products."

**Action:** show the dashboard, then the settings — accent colour, greeting,
position.

> "The merchant sets the colour and the greeting. There is nothing else to
> configure. It reads the catalog automatically."

---

## SCENE 2 — Turning it on (0:30–0:50)

**Screen:** Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds → toggle
StoreAgent on → Save

> "It installs as a theme app embed. One toggle, and it appears on every
> page of the store."

**Do not skip this scene.** The reviewer has to see how it is enabled.

---

## SCENE 3 — The core flow (0:50–1:50)

**Screen:** the storefront. Click the launcher.

**Type:** `can you show me some shoes`

> "It searches the merchant's live catalog. Real prices, real sizes, real
> stock."

*Wait for all four cards to finish rendering. Pause two seconds.*

**Type:** `best one`

> "Now watch this. I did not say the word 'shoes'. It remembers what we were
> talking about."

*This is the strongest moment in the video. Let it sit for three seconds.*

**Type:** `do you have it in white`

> "It switches to the white variant, and the picture changes with it."

**Type:** `flip flops`

> "The store does not sell anything called flip flops. It understood what I
> meant, and found the sandals."

---

## SCENE 4 — The honesty moment (1:50–2:10)

**Type:** `do you sell ski helmets`

> "When the store does not stock something, it says so. It never invents a
> product, and it never invents a price. Every price it shows is checked
> against the catalog before the customer sees it."

**Say this line clearly.** For an app reviewer it is the most important
sentence in the video.

---

## SCENE 5 — Close (2:10–2:30)

**Screen:** back to the admin dashboard.

> "The merchant can see how many conversations happened, and which ones led
> to a sale. StoreAgent measures its own effect on revenue."

---

## Voice — film as a separate clip

Do not put voice in the main take. If a turn fails you would have to record
everything again. Film it on its own and cut it in after Scene 3.

**Press the mic. Say:** "show me white sneakers"

> "It also listens. The customer can just ask."

**Speak close to the microphone.** Captured audio has been coming through
quiet, and quiet audio is what makes transcription fail. If you get "I
didn't catch that", that is the guard working — say it again, louder. It is
better than the alternative, which was answering a question nobody asked.

---

## Do not put these on camera

- Anything with a typo. `chepeapest one` searches gibberish.
- `I need something comfortable for walking all day` — long rambling
  sentences fall under the match threshold and return a snowboard.
- The cart, until inventory is set.
- `cheapest one` as the **first** message — across the whole catalog it lands
  on the $10 gift card, which is unavailable, and the answer gets awkward.

---

## Submission notes — paste alongside the video

> Test instructions: Enable the StoreAgent app embed under Online Store →
> Themes → Customize → App embeds. Open any storefront page and click the
> assistant launcher, bottom right. Ask about products in the catalog.
>
> The assistant answers only from the merchant's live product data and store
> policies. It does not answer from general knowledge, and any product claim
> it cannot verify against the catalog is withheld rather than guessed.
